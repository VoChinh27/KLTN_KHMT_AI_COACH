Do an xay dung mot he thong AI Coach phan tich dong tac the thao tu video.

keypoints, chuan hoa thanh cac dac trung ky thuat, so sanh voi reference
model, sau do tra ve diem so va phan hoi huan luyen.

Y tuong cot loi:
  - Khong danh gia truc tiep bang cam tinh.
  - Moi nhan xet phai dua tren so do tu pose/keypoint.
  - Moi mon the thao co the cau hinh rieng bang YAML.
  - Pipeline xu ly duoc ca chuyen dong tuan hoan, vi du chay bo, va chuyen
    dong roi rac, vi du xuat phat thap.

Trong pham vi hien tai, du an dang tap trung vao:
  - Mon "running": chay bo, co pha static va dynamic.
  - Mon "sprint_start": xuat phat thap va dap lao, gom pha static
    ("San sang") va sprint_start ("Boc phat").

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

File chinh:

Muc dich:
  - Bien video thanh chuoi PoseFrame.
  - Neu nhieu nguoi trong khung, chon nguoi chinh.

Dau vao:

5.3. Stage 3 - Feature Extraction
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

File chinh:
  - stage3_features/normalizer.py
  - stage3_features/anomaly_detector.py
  - stage3_features/cycle_segmenter.py
  - stage3_features/discrete_event_segmenter.py
  - stage3_features/feature_extractor.py

Muc dich:
  - Chuyen PoseFrame thanh dac trung ky thuat.
  - Phan biet frame hop le/bat thuong.
  - Xu ly rieng chuyen dong tuan hoan va chuyen dong roi rac.

Dau vao:
  - List[PoseFrame]
  - fps
  - phase: static, dynamic, hoac sprint_start

Xu ly trong normalizer.py:
  - Tinh goc khop: knee, hip, elbow, ankle, trunk, head.
  - Tinh ratio: shoulder_width, hip_width, thigh, shank, wrist_width,
    hand_ground_clearance, hip_above_shoulder, shoulder_forward.
  - Remap left/right sang front/back cho sprint_start.
  - Tinh trunk_thigh_angle.
  - Voi sprint_start, cac distance metric quan trong duoc normalize theo
    shoulder_width de phu hop tai lieu 2D monocular khong calibration.

Xu ly trong anomaly_detector.py:
  - Phan loai frame: STATIC, MOVING, TRANSITION, INVALID.
  - Co bo nguong rieng sprint_start_thresholds() de tranh coi tu the cui
    thap la nga.

Xu ly trong cycle_segmenter.py:
  - Dung cho dynamic/cyclic movement, vi du chay bo.
  - Tach chu ky, resample ve 101 diem, tinh cadence/ROM/symmetry.

Xu ly trong discrete_event_segmenter.py:
  - Dung cho su kien roi rac, vi du sprint_start.
  - Tim su kien boc phat, lay snapshot pre/at/post event.
  - Sinh key dang at_trunk, at_knee_front, post_knee_back...

Xu ly trong feature_extractor.py:
  - Gom cac buoc tren thanh FeatureBundle.
  - StaticPhaseSummary hien co angle_mean, ratio_mean, feature_mean.
  - DynamicPhaseSummary co cadence, ROM, symmetry, mean curve.
  - SprintStartPhaseSummary co launch_frame, peak_acceleration,
    angles_at_launch, event_features.

Dau ra:
  - FeatureBundle.


5.4. Stage 4 - Reference Model, Scoring, Feedback
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

File chinh:
  - stage4_scoring/reference_model.py
  - stage4_scoring/scorer.py
  - stage4_scoring/feedback_generator.py

Muc dich:
  - Xay khoang chuan.
  - Tinh diem so.
  - Sinh feedback text.

ReferenceModel:
  - Co the xay bang thong ke tu video HLV.
  - Co the xay bang fixed ranges tu giao trinh/tai lieu ky thuat.
  - Co the ket hop mixed.

Voi running:
  - Reference thuong xay tu du lieu HLV bang median_iqr hoac mean_std.

Voi sprint_start:
  - Reference chinh hien tai la fixed_reference_ranges trong
    config/sport_configs/sprint_start.yaml.
  - Pipeline.from_config co the tu dung ReferenceModel tu YAML neu chua co
    file JSON reference model.

Scorer:
  - Tinh z-score: (student_value - reference_center) / reference_spread.
  - Chuyen z-score thanh score 0-100 bang ham Gaussian.
  - Tong hop diem theo trong so feature_weights trong YAML.
  - Ho tro static, dynamic va sprint_start.

Feedback:
  - Sinh text dua tren ScoreReport.
  - Co display name tieng Viet cho feature.
  - Co advice override rieng theo sport config.
  - Khong tu tao so lieu moi; chi dien so vao template.

Dau ra:
  - ScoreReport.
  - FeedbackReport.
